# Combat sounds

Open **Sounds** in the top bar, choose **Female** or **Male**, then play **Attack**, **Exertion**, **Take damage**, or **Defeat**. Voices are selected explicitly; character appearance does not assign a voice.

The panel plays cues on your computer. It does not broadcast audio to the other seats or trigger automatically from combat rules. A new cue replaces one already playing. Stop ends it immediately. Voice volume and the selected voice are remembered in this browser. Sound on/off shares the existing dice mute switch.

The eight included clips are edited human recordings from Flare: Empyrean Campaign, with source attribution and the CC BY-SA 3.0 license in `client/public/audio/combat/`. The attack mixes a short vocal cue with a melee swish; exertion retimes a short grunt without changing its pitch. Damage and defeat use the matching original voice recordings. The files are bundled with the client and work after a normal Git pull/build; they require no microphone, API key, or external audio service.
