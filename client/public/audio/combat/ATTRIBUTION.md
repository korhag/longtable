Original recordings in `mods/fantasycore/soundfx/`:

- `female_hit.ogg` and `female_die.ogg`
- `male_hit.ogg` and `male_die.ogg`
- `melee_attack.ogg`

Longtable adaptations (2026) retain the same CC BY-SA 3.0 license:

| Cue | Changes from the source |
| --- | --- |
| Attack | Original matching voice's hit vocalization mixed with a quiet melee swish; short lead-in, gain adjustment and fades. |
| Exertion | Original matching voice's short hit vocalization slowed with pitch preservation; gain adjustment, fades and a short silence tail. |
| Take damage | Original matching voice's hit vocalization with gain adjustment, short fade-in and silence tail. |
| Defeat | Original matching voice's death vocalization with gain adjustment and fades. |

All clips are mono Ogg Vorbis at 44.1 kHz. A limiter prevents peaks above 0.88 amplitude. The voice sets use the original female and male recordings; they are not generated impersonations or gender pitch conversions.
